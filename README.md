# Snuggle Inn — preview (work in progress)

A temporary, work-in-progress test build of Snuggle Inn, a cozy hotel game for families. Things will change or break; this is not a release.

Play: https://chirowsan.github.io/snuggle-inn-preview/

Your progress is saved only in this browser, for this address. Best in landscape on a phone.

## What's new in Round 3

- **A livelier outside:** painted grounds and garden, day–night light, butterflies and strolling guests.
- **Venues on higher floors:** restaurant, salon and massage spa on 3F; music lounge and theater on 4F. The hotel grows taller outside, with glowing venue windows, a tall SNUGGLE INN sign and a rooftop concert terrace.
- **Garden friends:** little bug visitors you can help for tips.
- **Olive's tea visit:** a letter, three gentle prep steps and a seal.
- **Gentle monthly bills** and a **Manager's desk** (Team / Money / Inn).
- **Secret critic:** a disguised reviewer, a morning paper and press clippings.
- **Tink the engineer** (a later unlock): spots and fixes breakages for you.
- **Gentle blackout / leaky-pipe events:** swipe the breaker up or turn the valve. Nothing is lost.
- **Clearer controls:** turn handles with solid arrowheads, finish motions for activities, a footsteps toggle.
